### [Quad9&\#x27;s Withdrawal from France and the Rising Regulatory Pressure on DNS](https://blog.cloudflare.com/quad9-withdrawal-from-france/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Cloudflare published an analysis discussing Quad9&\#x27;s withdrawal from France as a critical warning sign regarding mounting legal pressure and overregulation of DNS infrastructure. The commentary highlights the growing regulatory risks faced by recursive DNS providers operating under strict national mandates. While industry observers and technical organizations express widespread concern, questions remain regarding how major providers like Cloudflare will respond if faced with similar legal rulings.

hackernews · subset · Nov 17, 22:13 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50538994)

**「Background」** Recursive DNS resolvers translate domain names into IP addresses for end users and have increasingly become targets for legal blocking orders aimed at copyright infringement and illicit content. Quad9 is a non-profit recursive DNS provider focused on security and privacy that has faced various legal challenges across European jurisdictions regarding blocking mandates.

**「Impact」** Internet service providers and DNS operators face heightened legal uncertainty and compliance burdens as European regulators increasingly hold core infrastructure providers liable for third-party content access.

**「Community Discussion」** Quad9 CTO johnhtodd reported receiving unanimous support at IETF 127 from peers regarding their stance, alongside a prevailing sense of industry dread over DNS regulation. Other commenters criticized the broader decline of European internet freedoms and questioned whether companies like Cloudflare would commit to similar withdrawals if unfavorable legal rulings target them.
